## Ahrefs / Semrush verification
1. Open Keywords Explorer and enter the exact primary keyword.
2. Record current volume, KD, traffic potential and Parent Topic.
3. Inspect the SERP overview for weak matches, forums, thin pages and low-authority results.
4. Review related terms and questions for secondary keywords.
5. Repeat using Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, UK and US databases where regional intent matters.
6. After launch, use Google Search Console → Performance → Search results → Queries.
7. Improve pages with high impressions and low CTR; create new pages only when Search Console shows a genuine gap.
8. Never treat keyword-tool volume as a traffic guarantee.
